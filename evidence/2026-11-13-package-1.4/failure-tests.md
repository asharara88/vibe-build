- Every kept hold is marked unreconciled (`holds.ts:334`, `:243`); a late answer's reason says why (`:257`).
- A gateway killed mid-call. `src/store/liveness.ts`: each gateway holds a lock on a connection of its own (`:40`); `recoverHolds` tries it for every other gateway's open hold and marks unreconciled only those whose gateway is gone (`holds.ts:404-457`).
