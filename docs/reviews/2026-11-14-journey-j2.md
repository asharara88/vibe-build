# Review of package J2, 14 November 2026

Written by Claude Code. Three fresh reviewers read J2 on the merged code (commit `a84b80c`: `claude/stage-1` with pull request #39 and the J2 branch merged in). None wrote the code and none saw its author's conversation. Each was given the design (`docs/design/journey-j2.md`), the author's evidence, the code and its change, the project rules, the build plan's review rules, the decisions of 13 and 14 November, and the open gaps on this area (130 to 139, 148 to 161, 173, 174).

By the build plan (section 3, step 6) and the design's section 8, two parts were reviewed adversarially (try to break it): part (a)'s path to the acceptance runner, and part (c)'s removal of containers at a stop. Everything else had one careful review.

**Result: no reviewer found a blocking defect in the code, and none found an existing check weakened, removed or edited.** "Blocking" means: it breaks a hard rule, or it leaves an item of "Done means" unmet. Two points blocked the *evidence* until runs were made on the merged code; both are answered by the runs of 14 November in `evidence/2026-11-13-journey-j2/`.

No serious finding was disputed, so no finding went to a second reviewer to be challenged. The fixes below were made after the review and are shown by tests and controls; they were not read again by a reviewer. That is one round of the two the plan allows.

Money: the step's reservation closes once, only after the engine's process has ended; J2 changed no reservation code. The pause comes only from the process's settings.
