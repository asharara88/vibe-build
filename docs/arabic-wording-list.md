## Suggested changes, for the owner to accept or reject

Found by fresh reviewers on 11 and 12 November 2026. Not yet applied: this package is a document only, and the suggestions wait on the owner. (The template's files were under the licence gate L11 of the build plan, which is true since 12 Nov 2026.) Once the owner accepts a suggestion, the change is made in `templates/next/messages/ar.json` and this list is regenerated.

| Key | Current Arabic | Suggested Arabic | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `app.sample_banner` | بيانات تجريبية. كل ما تراه هنا مُختلَق. | بيانات تجريبية. كل ما تراه هنا غير حقيقي. (or: وهمي) | "مُختلَق" carries the sense of a falsehood (fabricated, made up as a lie). "غير حقيقي" (not real) is the most neutral; "وهمي" is the common word for dummy data but is also used for fake things. The owner's call. |
| `common.confirm_no` | لا، عُد | لا، رجوع | "عُد" is grammatical but unusual on a button; "رجوع" is the usual form for a Back button. |

## Notes for the owner

- The template's role names (Employee, Manager, HR admin) are placeholders from test example 1; each generated app has its own roles, translated at build time.
- The sample-data banner appears on every page of every generated app in Stage 1 and must stay (house standard: sample data is labelled as sample data).
- The app's name, `Internal app` / `تطبيق داخلي`, is a placeholder until a project names its app.
- Messages for later features (confirmation before anything destructive, numbers with their source and period) are already translated so they are ready when those screens appear.
